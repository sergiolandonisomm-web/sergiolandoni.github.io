Patagonia in The Buyer

Two articles written for the UK wine trade publication The Buyer, exploring the development of Patagonian wine and the changing circumstances around Neuquén’s vineyards.

The first looks at Patagonia’s place in the international Pinot Noir market. The second considers how twenty-five years of winemaking experience in Neuquén may inform the next stage of its development. Both begin with the territory and the people working in it.

The Buyer · 2026-11-06

Why Patagonia’s Neuquén Is a Wine Region Worth Watching

Neuquén's modern wine industry developed around San Patricio del Chañar, where irrigation, investment and the work of growers established vineyards in an arid part of northern Patagonia. This article considers what that experience can tell us today, as the province studies new irrigation infrastructure along the Limay River and its wider economy changes around the energy sector. It also examines the practical limits: vineyard area, water access, costs and the time required to develop a viable wine project.

The Buyer · 2026-08-25

Why Patagonia Is Finding Its Place in a Changing Pinot Noir Market

Patagonia is attracting attention from wine professionals looking beyond the established sources of Pinot Noir. This article explores how the region's wines fit into that changing market, while recognising the considerable differences between Patagonia's growing areas. Neuquén's experience is part of a wider story about vineyards, climate, regional knowledge and the gradual emergence of a wine identity.

About the author

Sergio Landoni is a Territory-Based Sommelier and Neuquén Wine Ambassador based in San Martín de los Andes, Patagonia, Argentina. His writing explores the relationship between wine, geography, history and the decisions behind the development of wine regions.
